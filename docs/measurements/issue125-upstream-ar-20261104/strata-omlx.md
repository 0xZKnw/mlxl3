## 1. Native GDN kernel fusion: smallest next kernel experiment

**Real upstream mechanism.** oMLX has an AR B1/T1 Qwen4 path, not just speculative verification: [prework/norm-gate description and default](https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/4213c3284abc7b3746a91464a7af7ccc3732b759/omlx/patches/qwen35_gdn_prework.py#L23-L31), [whole-step kernel topology](https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/4213c3284abc7b3746a91464a7af7ccc3732b759/omlx/patches/qwen35_gdn_prework.py#L379-L395), [one-head512-thread launch](https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/4213c3284abc7b3746a91464a7af7ccc3732b759/omlx/patches/qwen35_gdn_prework.py#L1130-L1154), and [actual forward caller](https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/4213c3284abc7b3746a91464a7af7ccc3732b759/omlx/patches/qwen35_gdn_prework.py#L2034-L2051). Its guarded geometry is exactly input2560 / conv10240 / recurrent48×128×128 ([guards](https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/4213c3284abc7b3746a91464a7af7ccc3732b759/omlx/patches/qwen35_gdn_prework.py#L1580-L1599)). It moves intermediates to threadgroup memory between prework, recurrence and final norm/gate.

**Current native gap and minimal first trial.** [gpu/gdn.rs235–289](../../../native/src/gpu/gdn.rs#L235) still materializes q/k/v, deltaF16 and normedF32 across prepare, step, norm and gate kernels. Start only with **norm + sigmoid gate in one kernel**, leaving preparation/recurrence/state/projections untouched. Current formulas are [flash_gdn.metal61–76](../../../native/shaders/flash_gdn.metal#L61) and [flash_gdn_epilogue.metal31–36](../../../native/shaders/flash_gdn_epilogue.metal#L31): preserve native F32 gain/normed/product, exact reduction, precise rsqrt/exp, existing sigmoid formula and final F16 rounding. Structurally this can remove one6144-F32 scratch and one dispatch per GDN invocation (36 per row, including PLE’s internal GDN; 36×24,576=884,736 requested bytes per untraced row). That is neither physical-memory saving nor measured GPU time.

This is **kernel fusion**, not same-encoder grouping or another command-buffer join. L1/P13 already place these operations in their enclosing CB; saving dispatch/intermediate work is distinct from removing a wait.

**History and limits.** [opti.md20901–20909](../../../opti.md#L20901) preregistered T17's MLX sigmoid/product epilogue; [20974–20982](../../../opti.md#L20974) preserve its real underflow discrepancy (03f1 versus03f2), compiler ICE and diagnosis. The corrected T17 epilogue is already present; proposing that epilogue again would repeat implemented work. New here is fusing the *remaining native norm producer* with that canonical consumer, in native Rust/Metal B1 rather than the old MLX lazy graph/B1-B8 domain. Preserve [T17 reports](../issue59-t17-20261029/) and its exhaustive finiteHalf gates, subnormal/rounding-boundary products and mutations. Do not copy oMLX's norm literally: its [norm-gate355–375](https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/4213c3284abc7b3746a91464a7af7ccc3732b759/omlx/patches/qwen35_gdn_prework.py#L355-L375) materializes dtypeT; our independently qualified native contract materializes F32. Whole-step fusion is a larger follow-up only after the small boundary is qualified; occupancy and state-index ownership could lose.

Before future speed: actual fused-dispatch counter, independent component/exhaustive T17 boundary corpus, four untraced model steps with finite full logits/hidden/84states and L1 actual counters, continuation/rollback, real CLI; then a pre-registered MAIN32 screen against accepted L1. The current intrusive profile attributes joint HC/GDN commands, not individual norm/gate cost: no recoverable milliseconds are claimed.

## 2. Common demand work queue: missing native adaptation, lower priority

**Upstream and local contrast.** Strata workers pop jobs from shared urgent/prefetch queues [stream.cpp309–347](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/blob/ffa80d73133ea5c548aefe57889a16e8085a938f/metal/src/engine/stream.cpp#L309-L347). Our [ssd_reads.rs208–218](../../../native/src/ssd_reads.rs#L208) creates one bounded channel per worker, then [394–398](../../../native/src/ssd_reads.rs#L394) assigns entire records round-robin. A slow worker can hold queued records while another exhausts its assigned queue. A minimal future variant is one bounded common FIFO of the same whole-record owned Reads, same four workers, byte count, admission, absolute deadline, completion/lease publication and error cleanup. No pieces, extra reads, extra workers or third GPU wave are needed.

This is not a newly discovered algorithm. [T18a final history at opti.md1384–1390](../../../opti.md#L1384) records mixed general results/Static default, while [1451–1458](../../../opti.md#L1451) records the AR qualification; [post-P09 native-FFI history1273–1279](../../../opti.md#L1273) already ports a dynamic queue into the *old MLX asynchronous ColdU* path. Production still contains [DiskWorkQueue](../../../native/ffi/disk_work_queue.h#L26), used by [DiskReadBatch](../../../native/ffi/disk_read_batch.h#L134). The concrete missing adaptation is the independent, newer Rust `ReadService` used by full-native AR. Old gains cannot transfer to this backend or current32-token workload.

Current corrected native8 contains336MoE waits/887MoE command intervals across seven rows, but no worker IDs/queue-dequeue timestamps; record durations cannot prove that static assignment exposes critical-path idle time. Sharedqueue therefore ranks below the GDN boundary and root's finalMoE→consumer boundary. First deterministic check should hold one early read, prove another worker completes a later eligible job (fallback/static mutant must fail), then EOF/partial/EINTR/drop/deadline/generation recovery. Reuse original L2/model oracles with queueON/OFF before any MAIN32 timing. Scope remains a hypothesis, not a proposed automatic flag/default change.
